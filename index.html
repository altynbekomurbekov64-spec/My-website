About Karakol

Karakol is the fourth-largest city in Kyrgyzstan, located near the eastern end of Lake Issyk-Kul. It is a beautiful destination known for its stunning mountain scenery, hiking trails, and rich cultural heritage. Visitors can explore the famous Dungan Mosque, the Holy Trinity Cathedral, and the Karakol Sunday Market.


Whether you love hiking, skiing, or simply enjoying nature, Karakol is a perfect place for adventure and relaxation.

Top Places to Visit

Ala-Kul Lake

A stunning alpine lake at 3,500 meters. A popular trekking destination with breathtaking views.

Dungan Mosque

A beautiful wooden mosque built without a single metal nail. A unique architectural masterpiece.

Holy Trinity Cathedral

A historic Russian Orthodox church made of wood. Very beautiful and peaceful.

Karakol Ski Base

One of the best ski resorts in Central Asia. Perfect for winter sports enthusiasts.
